Overprint

Overprint: Stamp Puzzles

Build each picture with a few well-placed stamps.

●Coming soon on the App Store

See the idea in motion

Overprint is a visual logic puzzle about reproducing a target print. Choose each stamp’s position and rotation, then press every stamp once; later impressions cover earlier ink, so the order shapes the final picture.

Early puzzles make placement and rotation easy to read. Later prints overlap in clever ways, turning the picture into a set of choices about which stamp should go down first and which should finish the image.

Build The Print

  • Place and rotate each stamp on the paper
  • Press every stamp once to reveal the target picture
  • Plan overlaps because later ink covers earlier impressions
  • 100 carefully designed puzzles with growing spatial depth
  • Endless challenges after the main collection
  • Undo, restart, and helpful step-by-step hints
  • No timer—experiment at your own pace
  • Progress saved on your device
  • English, Spanish, French, Italian, and German
