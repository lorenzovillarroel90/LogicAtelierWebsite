Shaplet

Shaplet: Shape Puzzles

Make every little piece belong.

●Coming soon on the App Store

See the idea in motion

Shaplet is a hands-on shape puzzle about seeing how pieces can become one exact silhouette. Drag pieces onto the workbench, move them freely, rotate them in place and overlap them while you experiment. To solve the puzzle, every square must finish inside the target with no overlap.

Early shapes invite quick discoveries. Advanced silhouettes ask you to reason about rotation, negative space and several pieces at once.

Shape The Solution

  • True drag-and-drop placement on and around the workbench
  • Rotate and reposition pieces without sending them back to the tray
  • 100 carefully designed puzzles with increasing spatial depth
  • Endless challenges after the main collection
  • Undo, restart and helpful step-by-step hints
  • No timer—experiment at your own pace
  • Progress saved on your device
  • English, Spanish, French, Italian and German
