Rayfold

Rayfold: Laser Puzzles

Bend the light until every target glows.

●Coming soon on the App Store

See the idea in motion

Rayfold is a visual laser puzzle about direction, reflection and consequence. Turn the mirrors and watch every beam recalculate instantly. Each colored ray must reach its matching diamond while walls and other elements reshape the possible path.

The first puzzles make reflection intuitive. Advanced boards ask you to trace several possibilities ahead and understand how one mirror can affect the entire solution.
