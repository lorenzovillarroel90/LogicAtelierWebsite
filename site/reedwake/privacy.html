Privacy for Reedwake: River Adventure

Reedwake stores game progress and settings locally. It does not use accounts, ads, analytics or subscription services. This page describes the app and this website.

This website

This static website has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts. GitHub Pages may receive ordinary request logs under GitHub’s own policies. The website does not use those logs to identify you or build a profile.

What the app stores

Reedwake stores game progress, badges and settings locally on your device. No account is required, and game progress is not sent to a Reedwake server. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to normal operating-system backups.

One-time purchase

Apple and StoreKit process the optional non-consumable full-game purchase. The app receives the verified entitlement state; it does not receive or store payment-card details. The purchase unlocks the remaining seven regions, 42 story crossings, all eight Tide Trials, and the complete story and ending. The Spain base price is €3.99; other storefront prices are set by Apple.

Ads, accounts and analytics

Reedwake contains no advertising, account system, subscription, or analytics service. The app uses StoreKit for the optional one-time full-game purchase.

Progress and deletion

Game progress and settings stay on this device. Deleting Reedwake removes its locally stored data, subject to normal operating-system backups. If you unlock the full game, the entitlement can be restored through the in-game purchase sheet.
