Knotide

Knotide: Untangle Puzzles

Find calm inside a tangled web.

●Coming soon on the App Store

See the idea in motion

Knotide is a tactile visual logic game: drag the nodes until no two threads cross. Every move reshapes the whole web, so the cleanest solution comes from seeing relationships rather than sorting labels.

Simple layouts teach the idea in seconds. Advanced puzzles add denser connections and fewer obvious escapes, turning each untangled network into a satisfying spatial challenge.

Untangle At Your Pace

  • Direct node dragging with fluid thread movement
  • 100 carefully designed puzzles with increasing depth
  • Endless challenges after the main collection
  • Undo, restart and helpful step-by-step hints
  • No timer and no pressure
  • Progress saved on your device
  • English, Spanish, French, Italian and German
