Reedwake: River Adventure

Dive, dodge, relight the river

Wake the river, one crossing at a time.

Guide Nim through a pixel-art river adventure with two layers to explore. Hop across moving hazards on the surface, dive beneath the rush, and find safe places to come up for air. Root roofs and shallow shelves mean neither layer is a shortcut: read the river, choose your moment and change depth.

A LITTLE BELL, A BIG DIFFERENCE Use the eddy bell to hold hazards for a few precious beats. Scout both layers while time is paused, then plan your next move. Gentle pace slows the rhythm without taking away rewards; its records stay separate.

EIGHT REGIONS TO RELIGHT The complete game contains 48 story crossings across eight regions, plus eight optional Tide Trials. Gather three motes on each route, earn up to 168 badges, meet the river's keepers and restore its eight sun-trees. Checkpoint rescues help you try again, while local saves remember your journey.

A COMPLETE FREE OPENING Play all six crossings of Wakewater Gardens for free. A single permanent Full Game purchase unlocks the other seven regions, 42 remaining story crossings, all eight Tide Trials, and the complete story and ending. No ads, subscriptions, consumables or paid retries. Settings, Scout and Gentle pace remain available in the free opening. Restore Purchases is available, and the purchase supports Family Sharing.

PLAY YOUR WAY Switch between the on-screen analogue stick and D-pad. Use the four action buttons, a compatible controller or a hardware keyboard. Play in English, Spanish, French, German or Italian. No account is required; progress and settings stay on your device.

Help & support

Questions about Reedwake?

Find practical answers about controls, saved progress, the free opening and your full-game purchase.

How do I move Nim?
The in-game guide explains movement, depth changes and crossing hazards. Use the movement control to move one tile at a time; the A action changes depth when the route allows it.

D-pad moves Nim one tile per beat. Hold a direction to keep hopping. Reach the sun-tree at the far bank. There is no countdown or lives limit.

A dives or rises. Root roofs block the top. Shallow shelves block the bottom. Find their openings and change layers BEFORE crossing. A arrows beside lanes show the open layer.

Where is my progress saved?
Crossing progress, badges and settings are saved locally on this device. Reedwake does not require an account. Deleting the app removes locally stored data, subject to normal operating-system backups.

My purchase is pending or the store is unavailable. What should I do?
A pending purchase is waiting for authorization or completion; keep playing the free chapter and check again later. If the store cannot load, check your connection and retry. A verified purchase or restore unlocks the full game while preserving your progress.
