Help & support

Questions about Brassbird: Shadow Heist Puzzle?

Find practical answers about controls, saved progress, the free opening and your full-game purchase.

How do I play?
SELECT toggles a next-beat forecast assuming WAIT. Aim mode previews the exact throw instead. X waits; B undoes. START opens pause, hints, restart and options. Nothing moves while you read or aim. A: aim your shadow. D-pad: choose a direction; press it again for more distance, up to 3 tiles. Opposite reduces distance. A throws. B cancels. Y swaps and consumes the shadow. Stone blocks throws; gaps do not.

Where is my progress saved?
Progress, records and settings stay locally on this device. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to normal operating-system backups. No account or online leaderboard is required.
