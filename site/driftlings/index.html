Driftlings

Driftlings: Sliding Puzzles

Slide every friend into a matching burrow.

●Coming soon on the App Store

See the idea in motion

Driftlings is a spatial puzzle where every swipe moves all the little creatures in the same direction. Walls and other friends stop a slide; a numbered burrow welcomes its matching friend home.

Simple boards teach the shared movement. Later puzzles ask you to plan a sequence of slides, use one friend as a stopping point, and guide everyone around the walls.
