Laneweave

Laneweave: Traffic Puzzles

Plan the route. Perfect the timing.

●Coming soon on the App Store

See the idea in motion

Laneweave is a traffic logic game where direction alone is not enough. Rotate road pieces, choose each car’s route, visit every required stop and adjust departure timing so the whole system runs without a collision.

Early puzzles introduce clean, direct journeys. Advanced boards add shared cells, loops, detours and revisits, rewarding careful planning instead of simply choosing the shortest road.

Make Every Journey Work

  • Route planning and departure timing in one clear puzzle
  • Continuous collision checking while cars move
  • 100 carefully designed puzzles with increasing depth
  • Endless challenges after the main collection
  • Undo, restart and helpful step-by-step hints
  • No timer while you plan
  • Progress saved on your device
  • English, Spanish, French, Italian and German
