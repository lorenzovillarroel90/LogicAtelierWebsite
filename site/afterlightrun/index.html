Help & support

Questions about Afterlight Run: Ember Quest?

Find practical answers about controls, saved progress, the free opening and your full-game purchase.

How do I play?
Use the movement control to guide Juniper. A jumps, B dashes, X flares and Y pauses. Start pauses. Choose the leaderboard in the title menu and open it with A. New arcade runs always begin at the first route.

Where is my progress saved?
Local records and settings stay on this device. Current arcade runs and checkpoints are not saved for a later launch. Deleting the app removes local records and settings, subject to normal operating-system backups.
What is included for free?
The first world: three complete routes.
What does the full-game purchase unlock?
Unlock four more worlds, twelve more routes, the full story and ending. Replay the whole arcade campaign and compete with your local records.

One-time purchase · No ads · No subscription

How do I restore a purchase?
Open the in-game full-game purchase sheet and choose Restore purchases.
My purchase is pending or the store is unavailable. What should I do?
A pending purchase is waiting for authorization or completion; keep playing the free chapter and check again later. If the store cannot load, check your connection and retry. A verified purchase or restore unlocks the full game while preserving your progress.
