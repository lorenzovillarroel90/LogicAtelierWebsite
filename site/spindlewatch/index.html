Help & support

Questions about Spindlewatch: Hearth Defense?

Find practical answers about controls, saved progress, the free opening and your full-game purchase.

How do I play?
Y lists the exact fixed roster before you commit. Left/right inspects future waves; Y opens enemy dossiers. During battle A cycles 1x/2x/3x, B pauses and Y freezes the battle to inspect waves. Planning time never runs out.

Where is my progress saved?
Progress, records and settings stay locally on this device. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to normal operating-system backups. No account or online leaderboard is required.
What is included for free?
The first four story roads in region one, including its guardian. Replay them freely.

How do I restore a purchase?
Open the in-game full-game purchase sheet and choose Restore purchases.
My purchase is pending or the store is unavailable. What should I do?
A pending purchase is waiting for authorization or completion; keep playing the free chapter and check again later. If the store cannot load, check your connection and retry. A verified purchase or restore unlocks the full game while preserving your progress.
