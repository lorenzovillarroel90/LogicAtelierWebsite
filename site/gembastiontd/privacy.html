Privacy for Gem Bastion TD

How Gem Bastion TD handles game data, optional tips and requests for support.

Data collection What the app stores Optional tips This website Help & support Related policies

Data collection

Gem Bastion TD does not collect personal data or track you. The app has no account, advertising or analytics.

What the app stores

The app stores your saved expedition and settings on your device. Deleting the app removes that local data from the device. Device backups are managed through your device and Apple account settings.

Optional tips

If you choose to tip, Apple processes the purchase. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit to confirm the transaction and does not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles purchase history and refund requests.

This website

This static website has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts. GitHub Pages may receive ordinary request logs under GitHub's own policies. The website does not use those logs to identify you or build a profile.

Help & support

You can contact the developer through the link on the support page. GitHub processes information you choose to submit there under its own privacy policy. Reports are public, so leave out personal and payment details.
