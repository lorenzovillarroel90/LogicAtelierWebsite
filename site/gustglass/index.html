Gustglass: Flightpaths

Aim a little storm home

Find a path home for Mica, a little storm in a cracked seed shell.

Plan a repeatable flight through wide numbered wind rings. Set angle and power from a nest, launch, steer gently and brake in the air. Soft banks, branches, wind and water shape the route. Retry restores the same launch angle, power and weather phase; the blue trail and predicted dots help you adjust.

Help & support

Questions about Gustglass: Flightpaths?

Find practical answers about controls, saved progress, the free opening and your full-game purchase.

How do I play?
The road bends. Use LEFT / RIGHT to aim, UP / DOWN for power, A to launch. In flight, steer gently; hold B to feather-brake. Your brake refills only in a nest. In flight. B brakes. X retries the same launch angle, power and weather phase.

Where is my progress saved?
Progress, records and settings stay locally on this device. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to normal operating-system backups. No account or online leaderboard is required.

How do I restore a purchase?
Open the in-game full-game purchase sheet and choose Restore purchases.
My purchase is pending or the store is unavailable. What should I do?
A pending purchase is waiting for authorization or completion; keep playing the free chapter and check again later. If the store cannot load, check your connection and retry. A verified purchase or restore unlocks the full game while preserving your progress.
