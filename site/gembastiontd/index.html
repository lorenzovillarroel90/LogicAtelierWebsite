Gem Bastion TD

Draft five gems, build your own maze and defend the bastion for 50 waves. The full game is free, with no ads.

●Coming soon on the App Store

Maze-Building Tower Defense

Build a maze of gem towers and defend the bastion through 50 waves.

Each round, place five gems. Keep one, combine a matching pair or use a recipe to forge a special tower or a slate. The gems you leave behind turn into rocks. Use them to send ground enemies the long way around seven checkpoints, then start the next wave when you’re ready.

Experiment with eight gem families and six quality grades. Use walkable slates to add effects along the route, and pair towers that strengthen each other. Open the Codex to check recipes, study enemies and plan for ground, flying and boss waves.

Choose from four difficulties and play a single-player expedition offline. Save your run on your device and resume when you’re ready.

Every wave, difficulty and feature is free. There are no ads or accounts. Three optional, repeatable tips support the developer without unlocking content or affecting gameplay.

Help & support

How do I play?

Tap a tile and choose Place Gem. Place five gems, then keep one, combine a matching pair or follow a recipe. Tap Start Wave when your defense is ready. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom and tap Fit to see the whole board.

How do I save and resume?

Use Save & Menu from the pause menu, then Resume on the main menu to return to your expedition. Your saved run lives on your device. The solo game can be played offline.

Is the game free?

All waves, difficulties and features are free. You can send any of the three optional tips more than once. Tips support the developer and do not change your game. Apple handles payment and purchase history.

Contact support

Ask a question or report a problem. Include the game name, your device and what happened.

Reports are public and require a GitHub account. Leave out personal and payment details.
